# Privacy Policy — Temporary Laptop Modes

Effective date: 2026-09-05

Temporary Laptop Modes is designed to work locally on your Windows device.

## Data we collect

The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. It
does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, accounts, cloud sync, or network
services.

## Local data

While a temporary mode is active, the app saves a local recovery snapshot of
the current Windows power settings under:

`%LocalAppData%\TemporaryLaptopModes\active-mode.json`

This exists only so the app can restore your previous settings if it is closed
or interrupted. The snapshot is deleted after a successful restore.

## Windows power settings

The app changes Windows power settings only after a user selects a mode. It
restores the saved settings after the mode ends, when applicable.

## Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact the publisher using the support email
shown on the Microsoft Store listing.
